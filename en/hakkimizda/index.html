Op. Dr. Halil Ibrahim Ocalan

Specialist in Orthopedics and Traumatology. Born in 1984 in Kirkağaç, Manisa; originally from Çerkeş, Çankırı. Kırıkkale University Faculty of Medicine (2009); orthopedic residency in Gaziantep (2016). Niğde Training and Research Hospital (2016–2023); Gaziantep Private Deva Hospital since 2023.
